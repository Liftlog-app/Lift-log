# Brand guide

## One line

**Lift Log: the lifting app that coaches every set.**

## What makes it different

- Suggests the weight for your next set from your effort (RPE) and adjusts as you go
- Adjusts the whole day to how ready you feel (sleep, soreness, energy)
- Spots plateaus and tells you the fix
- Works around pain with joint-friendly swaps
- Race your past self set by set (ghost race)
- A real 15-week plan with Hypertrophy, Strength and Peak blocks, or your own program

## Voice

Like a knowledgeable training partner: short, direct, encouraging, gym-real. Specific numbers over hype ("+10 lb on bench in 6 weeks", not "insane gains"). No medical claims, no extreme dieting, no shaming bodies.

## Face of the brand

Nheil, a college lifter and electrical engineering student who built the app for his own training. Content should show the app in real gym moments: between sets, after a PR, on deload week.

## Visual

Dark charcoal with plate colours: Push red `#d8342c`, Pull blue `#2a63d4`, Legs yellow `#e0a800`, green `#1d9a5b` for wins. Type: Barlow Condensed for headlines, Barlow for text. Barbell icon with red, blue and yellow plates.
