_Not planned yet. The Product Lead fills this in at the start of each cycle._

## Now (next 2–4 weeks)

- **PR share cards**: one tap turns a new personal record into a branded image to post. Growth loop for Nheil's gym content. Needs a native share/save bridge in the Android and iPhone wrappers.
- **Exercise library**: browse all exercises with photos, the muscles they train and how-to steps (Free Exercise DB includes instructions and muscle data).
- **Bodyweight and cut tracker**: log morning weight, show the weekly average and the rate of loss against a goal date (Nheil starts a cut mid-February).

## Next

- Competition or event dates: plan peaking and tapering backward from a date
- Total training load: lifting + sport/cardio sessions and a this-week-vs-4-week-average warning
- Boxing mode: round timer for bag work that logs rounds
- Supersets and circuits
- Notes per exercise and per workout
- Export to CSV

## Later

- Hands-free voice logging (web version first)
- Wearable data (heart rate variability, sleep) feeding the readiness score
- Coach view for trainers with several athletes
- Video form check tied to a set

## Blocked (needs Nheil)

- **Accounts and cloud sync**: needs a free Supabase project. Nheil creates it at supabase.com and shares the project URL and public (anon) key.
- **Google Play release**: needs a Google Play developer account (one-time fee), a private release signing key, a privacy policy page and a 12-tester, 14-day closed test.
- **App Store release**: needs an Apple Developer Program membership (yearly fee).
- **Pro subscriptions**: after accounts and store accounts exist.
