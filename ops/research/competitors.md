## Strong

**Positioning:** fast, simple logger. No program generation.

- **Pricing:** free with unlimited workouts but only 3 custom routines; PRO is $4.99/mo or $29.99/yr, lifetime $99.99. [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/strong/id464254577) · checked 2026-11-24
- **Standouts:** full Apple Watch app that logs without the phone; warm-up and plate calculators; e1RM and volume charts; supersets, set tags; CSV export. [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/strong/id464254577) · checked 2026-11-24
- **Recent:** v6.6.0 (late Nov 2026) improved iPhone–Watch live sync, formatted notes, timezone per workout. [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/strong/id464254577) · checked 2026-11-24
- **Rating:** 4.9 from ~109K ratings. [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/strong/id464254577) · checked 2026-11-24
- **Praise:** easy to use, Watch app. **Complaints:** subscription cost; plate calculator assumes standard bars (no 25 lb Smith bar); rigid exercise database (can't rename built-ins, sort by movement pattern). [App Store reviews](https://apps.apple.com/app/strong/id464254577) · checked 2026-11-24
- **Forum view:** described as the go-to fast logger in lifting subreddits; criticised for basic graphs and the 3-routine cap. [Cora blog, paraphrased composites (competitor source)](https://www.corahealth.app/blog/best-workout-tracker-reddit) · checked 2026-11-24
- **Coaching:** Jefit's comparison says Strong does not generate plans. [Jefit blog (competitor source)](https://www.jefit.com/blog/fitbod-vs-strong-vs-jefit-vs-hevy) · checked 2026-11-24

## Hevy

**Positioning:** cheapest Pro, strongest social feed, big user base (claims 15M+ lifters).

- **Pricing:** free tier has unlimited logging, no ads, 4 routines, 7 custom exercises, 3 months of graph history; Pro $2.99/mo (a $3.99 SKU also exists), $23.99/yr, $74.99 lifetime. [SensAI (competitor source)](https://www.sensai.fit/blog/fitness-app-pricing-free-tier-comparison) · checked 2026-11-24
- **Hevy Trainer (Pro only):** questionnaire builds a program; shows one routine at a time; suggests when and how much to add weight. Help page does not mention RPE, readiness or deloads. [Hevy help](https://help.hevyapp.com/hc/en-us/articles/38385724273047-Hevy-Trainer-Explained-How-It-Builds-Your-Workout-Program) · checked 2026-11-24
- **Compare-to-last:** a "previous" column shows last time's numbers, from any workout or the same routine (setting). [Hevy help](https://help.hevyapp.com/hc/en-us/articles/34105442929943) · checked 2026-11-24
- **Body tracking:** logs bodyweight, body fat, circumferences, progress photos with a graph each; the page tells users to work out their weekly average themselves (no built-in trend). [Hevy features](https://www.hevyapp.com/features/track-body-measurements/) · checked 2026-11-24
- **Recent:** v3.2.7 added contact/discovery privacy settings and sled push distance. [App Store ZA](https://apps.apple.com/za/app/id1458862350) · checked 2026-11-24
- **Praise:** simple, flexible, light monetisation, PR notifications motivate. **Complaints:** no Garmin integration (store); app "heavier" over time and social feed divides users (forums). [App Store ZA](https://apps.apple.com/za/app/id1458862350), [Cora blog (competitor source)](https://www.corahealth.app/blog/best-workout-tracker-reddit) · checked 2026-11-24

## Fitbod

**Positioning:** auto-generated workouts from equipment and muscle recovery. Most "hands-off" option.

- **Pricing:** no free tier (7-day trial needs a card); $15.99/mo or $95.99/yr; store also lists $12.99/$79.99 and older legacy prices. [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/fitbod-gym-fitness-planner/id1041517543), [SensAI (competitor source)](https://www.sensai.fit/blog/fitness-app-pricing-free-tier-comparison) · checked 2026-11-24
- **Standouts:** non-linear periodization, workout modifiers, 1,000+ exercises with multi-angle videos, Strava/Fitbit/Apple Health. [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/fitbod-gym-fitness-planner/id1041517543) · checked 2026-11-24
- **Recovery map:** shows how fresh each muscle group is. [TechRadar, 2023 review](https://www.techradar.com/health-fitness/fitbod-app-review) · checked 2026-11-24
- **Injuries/limitations:** user marks an area (lower back, shoulder, knee); exercises that could stress it are excluded and alternatives preferred; says it is not medical advice. Closest competitor to our pain log. [Fitbod help](https://help.fitbod.me/hc/en-us/articles/37629269518103) · checked 2026-11-24
- **Rating:** 4.8 from 291K ratings (store); 3.0 on Trustpilot from 38 reviews. [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/fitbod-gym-fitness-planner/id1041517543), [Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/fitbod.me) · checked 2026-11-24
- **Complaints:** trial auto-converting to a yearly charge and hard cancellation (several 2025–26 Trustpilot reviews); "too expensive"; repetitive workouts; can't leave the workout screen mid-session; no single place to see PRs; shared workouts include promo text. [Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/fitbod.me), [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/fitbod-gym-fitness-planner/id1041517543) · checked 2026-11-24

## JEFIT

**Positioning:** older all-rounder, big library and community, now pushing an "Adaptive Plan".

- **Pricing:** free tier with ads; Elite $12.99/mo or $69.99/yr. [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/jefit-workout-plan-gym-tracker/id449810000) · checked 2026-11-24
- **Standouts:** Adaptive Plan (12 weeks in blocks incl. deload, adjusts from logged sets); periodization for custom plans added Sept 2026; 1,400+ exercises; recovery chart and muscle map; 700+ official and 20,000+ community plans. [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/jefit-workout-plan-gym-tracker/id449810000), [Jefit blog (competitor source)](https://www.jefit.com/blog/fitbod-vs-strong-vs-jefit-vs-hevy) · checked 2026-11-24
- **Rating:** 4.8 from ~47K. [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/jefit-workout-plan-gym-tracker/id449810000) · checked 2026-11-24
- **Complaints:** frequent updates that add prompts and remove features; hard to view one exercise's progress; bugs (wrong videos, missing logs); Watch timer freezes; dated UI per forum summaries. [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/jefit-workout-plan-gym-tracker/id449810000), [Cora blog (competitor source)](https://www.corahealth.app/blog/best-workout-tracker-reddit) · checked 2026-11-24

## Boostcamp

**Positioning:** free library of known programs (nSuns, GZCLP, PHUL) and coach plans, with a tracker.

- **Pricing:** library free, no ads; Pro $14.99/mo or $59.99/yr with 7-day trial. [Boostcamp auto-progression page](https://www.boostcamp.app/features/auto-progression) · checked 2026-11-24
- **Auto-progression (Pro):** adds weight after all targets are hit; double progression for rep ranges; after a missed set it estimates a lower hittable weight; a logged RPE above target *holds* the weight. No readiness input described. [Boostcamp](https://www.boostcamp.app/features/auto-progression) · checked 2026-11-24
- **Standouts:** 11,000+ programs; in-session PR alerts; weekly Sunday reports and year-end "Wrapped"; Lock Screen timers; AI Coach program builder. [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/boostcamp-gym-workout-fitness/id1529354455) · checked 2026-11-24
- **Recent:** v268 (Nov 6) community features + history calendar; v267 (Oct 28) AI support. [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/boostcamp-gym-workout-fitness/id1529354455) · checked 2026-11-24
- **Rating:** 4.9 from ~11K. **Complaints (older):** freezing after workouts, lost data on poor connection (offline mode added 2024), programs not advancing after an update. [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/boostcamp-gym-workout-fitness/id1529354455) · checked 2026-11-24

## JuggernautAI

**Positioning:** premium AI powerlifting/powerbuilding coach. Closest to Lift Log's "coach every set" idea, at many times the price.

- **Pricing:** store lists $43.99/mo; third-party sources say $34.99/mo or ~$349.99/yr with a 14-day trial (prices differ; store is the more direct source). [App Store CA](https://apps.apple.com/ca/app/juggernautai/id1515756471), [Alpha Progression blog (competitor source)](https://alphaprogression.com/en/blog/alpha-progression-vs-juggernautai) · checked 2026-11-24
- **Standouts:** readiness rating adjusts training before, during and between sessions; 0–100 readiness score; sleep and bodyweight tracking; meet-day tracking; weak-point exercise picks. v3.0.0 redesign (Aug 2026). [App Store CA](https://apps.apple.com/ca/app/juggernautai/id1515756471) · checked 2026-11-24
- **Autoregulation in practice:** daily 1–5 ratings for sleep, motivation, food, soreness per muscle; a reviewer saw squat load cut for one session due to quad soreness, and a set removed after an RPE 10. [Garage Gym Reviews](https://garagegymreviews.com/juggernautai-review) · checked 2026-11-24
- **Rating:** 4.6 from 398. **Complaints:** price; long onboarding before a paywall; loading bugs; can't mute rest timer; strength only, no cardio. [App Store CA](https://apps.apple.com/ca/app/juggernautai/id1515756471), [Garage Gym Reviews](https://garagegymreviews.com/juggernautai-review) · checked 2026-11-24

## Newcomer: MacroFactor Workouts

**Why it matters:** made by the team behind the MacroFactor nutrition app, which is known for its smoothed weight trend. It is the most likely app to join "cut" and "lifting" data.

- **Pricing:** no free tier, 7-day trial; $11.99/mo, $47.99/6 mo, $71.99/yr. [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/6737156524) · checked 2026-11-24
- **Standouts:** adaptive plans; Static/Linear/Reverse Linear/Undulating progression; RIR, myoreps, partials; weekly sets per muscle; Jeff Nippard technique videos. [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/6737156524) · checked 2026-11-24
- **Weight link:** the help centre suggests comparing your weight trend with workout data. A falling trend plus dropping volume and more fatigue may mean the deficit is too big. This is advice to the user, not an automatic feature. [MacroFactor help](https://help.macrofactorapp.com/en/articles/278-weight-trend-workouts) · checked 2026-11-24
- **Rating:** 4.8 from ~5K. **Complaints:** bugs/lost sessions; RIR poorly explained for beginners; suggested weights don't match the user's equipment; no data import. [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/6737156524) · checked 2026-11-24

## What this means for Lift Log (opinion)

- The cheap loggers (Strong, Hevy) record sets but don't coach them. The apps that do coach (JuggernautAI, Fitbod, MacroFactor Workouts) have **no free tier** and cost $72–$420+ a year. Lift Log has RPE suggestions, readiness, plateau fixes and pain swaps all in one place, which only JuggernautAI comes close to. That gap is our pitch.
- Price and billing anger is the most common complaint across apps (Fitbod trial charges, JuggernautAI price, Strong subscription). An honest, generous free tier is a marketing angle once Pro exists.
- No app we checked turns a bodyweight trend into an automatic training adjustment during a cut. See the deep dive `2026-11-24-cut-coaching.md`.
- Equipment-aware weights are a repeat complaint (Strong Smith bar, MacroFactor suggestions). Check that our plate math handles custom bar weights and the user's available plates.
